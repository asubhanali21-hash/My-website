Fabric Selection and GSM

Fabric affects how a hoodie feels, fits and performs. Cotton fleece is a common starting point for our custom hoodie orders, but the final selection depends on your requirements and material availability.

We discuss the fabric composition, weight, inside finish, colour and intended season before confirming the order.

Understanding GSM: GSM means grams per square metre. It describes fabric weight, but it does not measure quality on its own. Yarn, fabric construction, finishing and stitching also affect the finished garment.

If you already know your required GSM, include it in your enquiry. If you are unsure, tell us whether you want a lighter everyday hoodie or a heavier streetwear style, and we can discuss suitable options.

Custom colours, washes and specialist finishes are reviewed separately for availability and any additional minimum requirements.

Colours, Fits and Construction Details

We can review designs for black hoodies, grey hoodies, dark grey hoodies, pink hoodies and other colours according to your collection.

For example, a black zip up hoodie may require a matching zipper, ribbed cuffs and a particular hood shape. A grey hoodie for men may need a different body length, shoulder width and size chart.

Before sampling, we work through details such as:

Body length, chest width and sleeve measurements.

Hood shape, lining and number of layers.

04. Pockets

Kangaroo pockets or separate front pockets.

05. Ribbing

Cuff and hem ribbing.

06. Trims & Hardware

Drawstrings, eyelets and zipper options.

07. Brand Identity

Logo size, placement and decoration method.

These specifications help turn a reference image into a product that can be sampled and manufactured.

Our Hoodie Manufacturing Process

A structured, transparent path from design review to final dispatch.

01
Stage 01

01. Design Review and Consultation

Send your tech pack, sketches or reference images. We review the style, quantity, size range, fabric preference, branding and delivery destination.

If you do not have a tech pack, we first identify the measurements and construction details needed to prepare a sample.

02
Stage 02

02. Fabric and Material Selection

We discuss suitable fabric options, GSM, colours and trims. This includes ribbing, drawstrings, zippers and labels where required.

Material availability and any special sourcing requirements are checked before the specifications are finalised.

03
Stage 03

03. Pattern Development and Cutting

The hoodie pattern is developed around the agreed measurements and fit. Fabric panels are cut for the body, sleeves, hood, pockets and other components.

The pattern establishes the shape of the garment and provides the basis for sampling.

04
Stage 04

04. Printing or Embroidery

Artwork dimensions, colours and placement are reviewed. Depending on the design and decoration method, printing or embroidery may take place on cut panels or on the assembled garment.

05
Stage 05

05. Sample Manufacturing and Review

A sample allows you to review the fit, fabric, construction and branding before bulk production.

You can identify changes to measurements, logo placement or finishing. Sample costs, revisions and shipping are quoted separately.

06
Stage 06

06. Bulk Hoodie Production

After sample and specification approval, the bulk order moves into production. The garments are stitched and assembled according to the agreed requirements.

Changes requested after approval are reviewed for their effect on cost and timing.

07
Stage 07

07. Quality Checks and Finishing

The finished hoodies are checked against the approved specifications. Checks cover measurements, stitching, decoration placement, visible defects and finishing.

Loose threads are trimmed and the garments are prepared for packing.

08
Stage 08

08. Labels, Packaging and Dispatch

Hoodies are labelled, folded and packed according to the agreed order requirements. Shipping arrangements are confirmed using the destination, parcel weight, dimensions and available service options.
