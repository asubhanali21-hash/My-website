## ⚙️ 5. Manufacturing Process (Strict 8-Step Timeline)

- **01 Design & Consultation:** We start with your concept, references or tech pack and align on scope.
- **02 Fabric & Material Selection:** Sourcing the right fabric weight, composition and finish for your product.
- **03 Sampling:** A physical sample is produced for your review and approval before bulk.
- **04 Pattern & Cutting:** Patterns are graded and fabric is cut to precise specification.
- **05 Production:** Garments move through sewing and construction on the production floor.
- **06 Quality Control:** Every batch is checked against approved samples and specifications.
- **07 Packaging:** Finished garments are folded, tagged and packed to your requirements.
- **08 Shipping:** Completed orders are prepared and shipped to your destination.
